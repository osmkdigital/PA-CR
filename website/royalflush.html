The Game

How Royal Flush works

Royal Flush is an iGaming platform built as the world's first fully crypto lottery — every ticket purchase and every payout runs through a crypto payment gateway end to end, no fiat step in between. Under the hood, every ticket is still dealt a five-card hand: when the clock hits 3:00 PM, hands are revealed in order and the strongest hand left standing takes the pot, ranked exactly like standard poker hand rankings, top to bottom. Buy a ticket, and you're not just entering the draw — you're stepping into the Elite family, the community of players growing alongside RF as it works to become a serious currency for gaming transactions.
